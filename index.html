Executive Summary

Three Matching Lots, One Block West
Three 7,500 SF lots on Huron Avenue, in the same zone, sold for $1,562,500 to $1,835,000
3816, 3832 and 3940 Huron Ave are the same size as this lot and carry the same Medium Density Multifamily zoning. They closed between November 2024 and November 2025 at $208 to $244 per land SF.

3837 College Avenue is a 7,499 SF lot (50 x 150 ft) in Culver City's RMD, Medium Density Multifamily zone, which allows up to 50 units per acre. It is improved with a 1947 three-bedroom house of about 1,017 SF and a rear dwelling the owner reports was built without permits. The owner reports the tenants have moved out, so a buyer can plan demolition or renovation with no tenancy to resolve.

The site's highest and best legally permissible use is new multifamily housing: about 8 to 9 for-sale townhomes or 8 apartments by right, or about 14 units with a State Density Bonus. The closest land sales support a value of $1,600,000 to $1,700,000. We recommend a list price of $1,600,000 ($213 per land SF) with a firm call for offers.

Location

1
An apartment block in Culver City. The lot sits one block south of Venice Boulevard in the Clarkdale tract, where most 7,500 SF lots already hold apartment buildings of six to eight units. Sony Pictures Studios and the Culver Center shopping district are less than a mile east.
2
Very walkable. Walk Score rates the block 87 ("Very Walkable"), with a Transit Score of 59 and a Bike Score of 74. La Ballona Elementary is about two blocks away.
3
Deep rental and buyer demand. In ZIP 90232 about 62% of households rent, the median household income is about $122,000 and the median home value is about $1.57 million (2024 American Community Survey). HUD's 2027 Small Area Fair Market Rent is $4,040 for a two-bedroom and $5,130 for a three-bedroom.

Highest and Best Use

A buyer will pay for what can be built here. Four pathways apply; the first three remove the existing structures. Unit counts are planning-level estimates on 7,499 SF, for the buyer's architect and land-use counsel to confirm.

How to read these: Pathways A and B are the realistic highest and best use, and the buyers for them set the price. Developer Interest

1
Who buys this lot. Small-lot and townhome builders (Pathway A), small apartment builders who hold what they build (Pathway B), and 1031 exchange buyers looking for a Westside development site. Owner-users are a backup pool at a lower price.
2
What the last campaign showed. Listed from May 2026 at $2,000,000 and then $1,800,000, the property drew more than ten requests for the offering memorandum and several developer calls, but no offer at that price. Two things held buyers back: the ask was above where these lots trade, and the house was occupied.
3
What has changed. The tenants are out, so a buyer can close and start design right away, and the price can now meet the market at $213 to $227 per land SF. Builders who looked earlier are the first calls.

The best evidence is the three same-size lots on Huron Avenue, one block west, in the same zone: $1,562,500, $1,600,000 and $1,835,000, an average of $222 per land SF. Each of those buyers also got a duplex or triplex with rent. This lot offers a vacant site, which a builder values, and a house and rear unit that need substantial repair, which a builder will remove. On balance we place its value at $1,600,000 to $1,700,000.

We recommend listing at $1,600,000, the low end of that range and $200,000 under the prior ask, with a call-for-offers date about three weeks after launch. Pricing at the market rather than above it is what brings several builders to the table at once, and competition among them is the best way to push the final price toward the top of the range.

Marketing Plan for Developers

1
Lead with the right facts. A one-page feasibility summary on the RMD zoning: about 8 to 9 townhomes, 8 apartments by right, or about 14 units with a density bonus. Builders price what they can verify.
2
Call the builders directly. Phone and email outreach to townhome and apartment builders active in Culver City, Palms and Mar Vista, including the buyers of the comparable lots above and the developers who requested the offering earlier this year.
3
Have the diligence ready. A preliminary title report, the trust paperwork showing who signs, any survey or plans on hand, and a plain disclosure of the unpermitted rear dwelling, so a buyer can shorten contingencies.
4
Run a short, firm process. Ten listing platforms, our developer and broker database, and a call-for-offers date. Any offer already on the table is presented side by side with the rest, compared on price, deposit, timing and certainty.
